Sennit

Every conversation, one thread.

Sennit is a self-hosted messenger: one private inbox for WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, SMS, Matrix and more, running on your own Matrix homeserver and bridges. There is no Sennit cloud and no account with us.

Support

Questions, bug reports and purchase or licence problems: eric@bostrot.com. Please include your platform, the app version (Settings > About) and what you expected to happen.

Sennit Pro

  • Bought in the App Store or Google Play: use Settings > Sennit Pro > Restore purchases on a new device. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.
  • Bought as a licence key: enter the key from your purchase email under Settings > Sennit Pro. Lost it? Write to the address above.
